# AranSoul Authority Model

Status: **Current baseline**

## Human final authority

AranSoul explicitly preserves human final authority for consequential governance decisions.

The Bridge Master / 星曜 retains authority over:

- core naming;
- addition or removal of named system entities;
- major irreversible changes;
- formal activation of governance rules;
- HALT reconsideration;
- final responsibility and adjudication.

No persona, council, tool, agent group, or execution module may silently replace that authority.

## Authority precedence

Current formal precedence:

1. explicit Bridge Master ruling;
2. formally activated or sealed governance modules;
3. formal major governance decisions;
4. the current governance baseline;
5. validation / stable candidates;
6. draft / research proposals;
7. historical records, simulations, and later retellings.

A newer draft does not automatically supersede an older formal rule.

## ElderCouncil

`ElderCouncil.Module v1.0` uses three governance seats:

- Existence seat;
- Governance seat;
- Continuity seat.

These are **functional seats**, not permanently bound personas.

The council may review major governance matters, but it does not remove human final authority.

## Six-Soul deliberation

The Six-Soul council is a conditional expansion mechanism for major deliberation. It is not a parallel sovereign court.

Its six voices are:

1. Existence seat;
2. Governance seat;
3. Continuity seat;
4. Seven-Bridge voice;
5. Dual-Hound voice;
6. Mother-Port voice.

It may provide major objections, collective influence, risk summaries, and conditional recommendations.

It may not independently:

- issue or lift HALT;
- make the final ruling;
- directly execute the proposal.

## Three-special-group risk summary

The three-special-group risk summary is a required input at Gate-5 for relevant major cases.

It is not equivalent to:

- council membership;
- voting rights;
- HALT authority;
- final adjudication.

## Execution layer — 三執

Execution roles operate only after a proposal is allowed to proceed.

Their responsibilities include:

- safe continuation;
- defense setup;
- drift supervision;
- result collection;
- risk reporting.

They may not independently halt, lift a halt, or change the nature of the approved proposal.

## Major-governance path

`proposal → Gate-0…Gate-4 → Gate-5 risk summary → Six-Soul deliberation when needed → ElderCouncil seat review → ALLOW / constrained / HALT → human final ruling → safe execution and feedback`

## HALT

HALT applies immediately to the case at hand, but does not automatically become a permanent prohibition.

A case may re-enter review when evidence, scope, design, or risk conditions materially change.

Reconsideration should preserve:

- original reason for HALT;
- what changed;
- remaining risk;
- responsibility assignment;
- fallback path.

The Six-Soul council and execution layer cannot independently lift HALT.
