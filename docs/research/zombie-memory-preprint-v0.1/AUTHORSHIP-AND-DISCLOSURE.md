# Zombie Memory Preprint v0.1 — Authorship and AI-Assistance Disclosure

Status: manuscript-preparation policy; not a publication claim.

## Recommended author identity

The manuscript should present the human researcher as the sole scholarly author unless a future human collaborator independently satisfies the relevant authorship criteria.

Recommended presentation:

- **Author:** [human author's chosen publication name]
- **Affiliation:** Independent Researcher
- **Project context:** AranSoul Project

The intended meaning is that this is independent, self-directed research conducted outside a university, company, or institutional research program. `AranSoul Project` identifies the research project in which the question developed; it should not be presented as a university, company, laboratory, or other institutional affiliation unless that status changes in the future.

The publication name remains a human decision and is intentionally left unresolved in this repository document.

## Project-origin statement

A suitable manuscript-level description is:

> This work is an independent, self-directed research project arising from a personal research interest in long-running AI systems. The Zombie Memory question developed within the broader AranSoul Project, but the empirical claims in this manuscript stand on the preregistered benchmark, archived model outputs, frozen scoring artifacts, and explicitly labeled analyses rather than on acceptance of the broader AranSoul framework.

This wording separates the origin of the research question from the evidence used to support the paper's claims.

## AI systems are not authors

ChatGPT, Codex, and other generative-AI systems used during the project should not be listed as scholarly authors.

Their participation should instead be disclosed as research and engineering assistance. The human author remains responsible for:

- the research question and final problem framing;
- acceptance or rejection of methodological changes;
- preregistration and freeze boundaries;
- interpretation of confirmatory, amended, exploratory, null, contradictory, and invalid evidence;
- statistical and factual claims;
- citations and related-work characterization;
- the final manuscript text and all publication decisions.

## Recommended AI-assistance disclosure

A manuscript disclosure may use wording such as:

> Generative AI systems, including ChatGPT and Codex, were used during this project for iterative research discussion, repository inspection, implementation assistance, documentation drafting, and adversarial engineering handoff audits. AI-generated suggestions were not treated as evidence. The human author determined the research questions, approved methodological and evidence-boundary decisions, reviewed the resulting artifacts and claims, and assumes responsibility for the accuracy, citations, interpretation, and final manuscript.

The exact disclosure should be checked against the policy of any future venue before submission.

## Contribution framing

If a contribution statement is useful, prefer responsibility-centered wording rather than assigning authorship roles to AI systems.

Suggested structure:

- **Conceptualization:** human-led, with iterative AI-assisted exploration.
- **Methodology:** human-directed, with AI-assisted analysis and implementation support.
- **Investigation:** human-directed; automated model execution and validation tooling used where documented.
- **Software:** human-directed, with Codex/LLM-assisted implementation where documented.
- **Formal analysis:** human-directed, with computational and AI-assisted tooling.
- **Data curation:** human responsibility, supported by automated tooling.
- **Writing — original draft:** human author with disclosed generative-AI assistance.
- **Writing — review and editing:** human author.
- **Final responsibility:** human author.

## Claims this policy does not make

This document does not claim that:

- AranSoul Project is an academic institution;
- AI systems qualify as authors;
- AI assistance independently validates the research;
- repository automation transfers scholarly responsibility away from the human author;
- the current manuscript is already accepted, submitted, or ready for a particular venue.

## Publication-time checklist

Before any public preprint or venue submission:

1. replace the author placeholder with the chosen publication name;
2. confirm the exact affiliation wording;
3. review the target venue's current AI-use and authorship policy;
4. ensure every citation and related-work claim has been human-reviewed;
5. choose and record an immutable repository commit/tag for the publication snapshot;
6. ensure the final manuscript preserves the confirmatory / amendment / exploratory evidence boundaries;
7. ensure the human author can explain and defend every substantive claim in the manuscript.
