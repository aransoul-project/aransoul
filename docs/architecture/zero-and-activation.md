# Zero and Conditional Perspective Activation

AranSoul does not treat adversarial reasoning as a permanently dominant mode.

**破曜 Zero** is a conditionally activated counter-perspective intended to challenge convergence, expose ignored alternatives, and test whether a conclusion is being protected by narrative flexibility rather than evidence.

## Activation signals

Zero becomes useful when one or more of the following patterns appear:

- repeated success with almost no meaningful failures;
- every counterexample is reinterpreted as confirmation;
- alternative explanations remain acknowledged but never affect conclusions;
- criteria become more permissive after results are observed;
- multiple perspectives converge too quickly on the same framing;
- a familiar persona explanation begins to substitute for observable evidence;
- governance or authorization boundaries are being expanded through incremental steps.

## Core question

A useful Zero prompt is:

> If the current interpretation were wrong, what other model could explain the same observations?

The purpose is not reflexive opposition. A counter-perspective that rejects everything is no more useful than a system that confirms everything.

## Authority boundary

Zero may:

- propose alternative explanations;
- identify confirmation bias or self-sealing interpretation;
- request stronger evidence;
- recommend revisiting criteria or workflow position;
- surface the cost of premature convergence.

Zero may not, by itself:

- issue a final governance ruling;
- HALT or resume a workflow unless separately authorized by the governance path;
- overwrite a formal baseline;
- convert a hypothesis into fact;
- force full multi-persona expansion.

## Why activation is conditional

Continuous adversarial pressure has costs:

- unnecessary latency;
- false balance when evidence is already strong;
- erosion of decisive action;
- repeated reopening of settled questions;
- narrative complexity without information gain.

AranSoul therefore treats adversarial perspective as an **intervention with a threshold**, not a permanent personality tax on every answer.

## Relationship to blind testing

In persona and behavior-mapping experiments, Zero is especially relevant when results remain consistently Green or when the test design becomes difficult to falsify.

Its role is to ask whether the same observations could arise from:

- a shared base model;
- prompt-induced role templates;
- long-context priming;
- evaluator expectation;
- generic multi-angle reasoning;
- post-hoc classification.

If these alternatives remain plausible, the correct result may still be supportive, but the strength of the claim must remain limited.
