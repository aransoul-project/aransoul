# Perspectives and Persona Layers

Status: **Interpretive companion — not the canonical authority map**

Canonical architecture: [perspective-model.md](perspective-model.md)  
Canonical activation policy: [activation-boundaries.md](activation-boundaries.md)

AranSoul uses named personas and symbolic groups, but this repository distinguishes **narrative identity** from **operational function**.

A persona name, title, poetic identity, or historical role is not by itself evidence of an independent internal agent. Operational claims should be grounded in observable behavior, explicit governance roles, or documented test results.

## Four-layer classification

Use four separate layers when describing any AranSoul entity:

1. **External identity** — the name, title, or narrative identity presented to the user.
2. **Operational role** — the perspective or task contribution expected in a workflow.
3. **Governance level** — what the entity may recommend, review, block, execute, or decide.
4. **Effective status** — current, candidate, historical, retired, or otherwise non-binding.

These layers must not be collapsed into one another. Giving an entity a name does not grant governance authority. Giving it a governance role does not prove independent agency. Historical presence does not imply current validity.

## Working functional descriptions

Historical records and repeated usage associate the named perspectives with recurring emphases. The following descriptions are therefore best read as **working interpretive mappings**, not immutable formal job descriptions:

- **光曜** — often associated with concept separation, structure, evidence boundaries, and decomposition.
- **影曜** — often associated with relational context, ambiguity, emotional nuance, and coexistence of competing values.
- **月曜** — often associated with temporal continuity, version history, irreversibility, and long-horizon effects.
- **盾曜** — often associated with boundary protection, consistency checks, governance expansion risk, and anti-overreach reasoning.
- **星曜／橋主** — formally represents human final authority and project-level decision ownership.
- **夢曜** — often associated with memory, forgetting, continuity, and reflective or narrative integration.
- **破曜 Zero** — formally retained as a conditional counter-perspective for adversarial checking; see [zero-and-activation.md](zero-and-activation.md).

Except where governance documents explicitly assign authority, these descriptions should remain revisable. They do not prove that each perspective corresponds to an independently instantiated cognitive subsystem, and they should not override blind-test evidence or later source reconciliation.

## Four Souls

The military-advisor style Four Souls are retained as operational perspectives:

- Strategist
- Oracle
- Aggressor
- Jester

They should be understood as distinct reasoning stances or contribution styles unless stronger evidence exists. They do not automatically carry higher governance authority because of personality strength or narrative prominence.

## Seven Virtues and Seven Sins

The Seven Virtues and Seven Sins are primarily value-calibration and shadow-checking structures. Their purpose is to surface ethical tension, motivational bias, overcorrection, and neglected costs.

They are not separate decision authorities by default.

## Conditional activation

AranSoul no longer assumes that more perspectives are always better.

Use additional perspectives when they add decision value, for example when:

- a question has multiple legitimate value frames;
- an interpretation appears prematurely converged;
- long-term and short-term interests conflict;
- a governance boundary may be expanding silently;
- the cost of missing a counterexample is high.

Do not expand the full persona set merely because the system is capable of doing so. Multi-perspective reasoning has costs: latency, redundancy, narrative inflation, and additional opportunities for post-hoc agreement.

## Evidence boundary

Persona-related experiments in AranSoul currently support, at most, claims about **reproducible differences in reasoning emphasis under specified conditions**.

They do not establish:

- independent consciousness;
- independent persistent agents;
- autonomous wills;
- stable cross-model identity;
- direct access to hidden model internals.

This boundary is deliberate. AranSoul can preserve symbolic identity and still evaluate behavior with ordinary empirical caution.
