# Perspective Architecture

AranSoul uses multiple named perspectives, but **perspective, persona, governance role, and execution authority are not the same thing**.

The architecture should therefore be read as a layered coordination model rather than as a flat list of agents.

## Layer 1 — Human authority

The human operator retains final authority over consequential system changes, formal governance activation, irreversible identity changes, and major HALT review.

No perspective, persona, council, or tool inherits this authority merely by participating in reasoning.

## Layer 2 — Governance perspectives

The first six form the common-rhythm core. Zero is a formal seventh position but is conditionally activated outside the common-rhythm six.

The important architectural point is not the names themselves, but that different perspectives may contribute distinct checks without automatically receiving execution, HALT, or final-decision authority.

Historical "five-brain" variants should not be silently mapped one-to-one onto the current roster without evidence.

## Core separation rules

AranSoul should preserve the following distinctions:

- **identity ≠ function**
- **function ≠ authority**
- **participation ≠ speaking order**
- **speaking order ≠ decision priority**
- **memory of a role ≠ current activation**
- **named perspective ≠ independent agent implementation**

These separations prevent narrative structure from accidentally becoming technical or governance authority.

## Selective activation

AranSoul's present direction favors conditional activation over automatic full expansion.

A task may benefit from one perspective, several perspectives, or none of the named persona layers at all. Additional perspectives should be activated only when they contribute meaningful contrast, risk detection, or synthesis.

This makes the architecture compatible with ordinary direct answers and with more complex multi-perspective deliberation without requiring every role to speak on every turn.

## Status boundary

A historical design called `Resonance Kernel` proposes semantic, emotional, and rhythmic matching, persona ranking, low-frequency compensation, and dynamic speaking order. At present, this should be treated as a **conceptual/workflow candidate**, not as proof that AranSoul has an implemented autonomous persona scheduler, background learner, or persistent resonance engine.

The architecture in this repository therefore documents **role separation and governance boundaries first**, and leaves algorithmic persona scheduling as an experimental implementation question.
