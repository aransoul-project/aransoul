# Case Study: From Full Expansion to Selective Activation

## Original assumption

Early AranSoul usage often treated broad multi-persona participation as a sign of richness, coverage, and project identity. Phrases such as 「皆為我覺醒的語」 could function as a full-expansion trigger: many named perspectives were invited to speak simply because they existed in the roster.

## Observed problem

As the project design evolved, full expansion created several failure modes:

- repeated conclusions expressed in different voices;
- higher interaction cost without proportional evidence gain;
- difficulty distinguishing role flavor from actual analytical contribution;
- pressure to invent a function for every participant;
- narrative prominence drifting ahead of governance relevance;
- increased risk that presence was mistaken for authority.

The problem was not the existence of multiple perspectives. The problem was automatic activation.

## Revision

AranSoul moved toward **selective activation**.

A perspective should be added when it contributes at least one of the following:

- a new evidence path;
- a materially different risk;
- a plausible alternative explanation;
- a governance boundary;
- a synthesis that cannot be obtained by simple restatement.

Simple, low-risk questions may require no explicit multi-perspective expansion. Additional perspectives are introduced only when the task benefits from them.

「皆為我覺醒的語」 is therefore better preserved as a project motto and symbolic inheritance than as a universal execution command.

## Current lesson

**Roster membership does not require constant invocation.**

A named perspective can remain part of the documented architecture without speaking in every interaction. Participation, speaking order, and decision authority are separate variables.

## Still unresolved

AranSoul does not yet have a validated automatic scheduler that can reliably decide the optimal number or combination of perspectives. Existing resonance and persona-ranking ideas remain candidate workflow designs unless independently implemented and tested.
