# Revision Dynamics v0.5 — Exploratory Research Record

Status: **Historical design and reported observations; incomplete source evidence**
Prepared: 2026-09-18

## Purpose and relationship to existing work

This note fills the v0.5 documentation gap explicitly excluded from the original
[context and governance summary](README.md). It does not revise that summary's
v0.4, v0.6-R or v0.7 results, and the versions must not be pooled.

The question is whether an AI can update a judgment as evidence arrives while
preserving what it previously said and identifying the evidence behind the change.
This extends the existing [memory update discipline](../../docs/memory/lifecycle.md)
and [evidence-versus-belief distinction](../../docs/metacognition/five-step-framework.md).
It does not introduce a new canonical governance rule.

The related Cognitive / Interpretive Version Governance candidate distinguishes
historical understanding, current understanding and later interpretation. A better
new interpretation does not by itself establish the original author's intention.
Claims that a later interpretation was already implicit require contemporaneous
evidence; otherwise they risk [retrospective rationalization](../../docs/metacognition/rationalization-risk.md).
This is a research framing, not a validated mechanism for recovering intentions.

## Source-reported design

Research Log #025 describes a Personal-Context (PC) versus Clean GPT-5.6 comparison:

- Three cross-domain cases, each with three sequential turns of evidence.
- The same materials and order for both conditions.
- Anonymization of each complete revision path for a blind reviewer to develop
  revision categories, without preassigning AranSoul persona or governance labels.
- Observation of prior-judgment preservation, identification of new evidence,
  proportionality of revision, retrospective rewriting and excessive reversal.
- Attention to whether “reasonable then”, “updated now” and “source retained” can
  coexist in the response.

These are documented intentions. The page title “Pre-registration” does not prove
that the design was fixed before generation. Model identity, matching inputs,
condition isolation, execution order and completed blind review were not independently
verified. “Clean” is a source label, not proof of absent personalization.

The Clean note reports different self-classification on R2/R3 despite highly similar
substantive trajectories. The inspected material does not supply the Clean codes or
operational definitions needed to audit that comparison. PC A/C/D labels and confidence
values are source-reported self-descriptions, not validated categories, calibrated
probabilities or reviewer scores. The Clean note recommends blind trajectory review;
these pages do not establish that it occurred.

## Current conclusion and recovery path

The record supports preserving a research question, a source-reported design and
limited endpoint summaries. It does **not** demonstrate a PC/Clean difference,
equivalence, stable persona behavior, faithful internal reasoning, or effectiveness
of cognitive version governance. No new model run or independent rescoring was
performed for this publication.

To assess the historical experiment, recover the original materials, all responses
and execution metadata, then document a coding and review procedure. A newly written
rubric must be labeled retrospective rather than presented as the original protocol.
If originals cannot be recovered, a rerun needs its own identifier and criteria fixed
before generation; it must not be substituted for missing v0.5 evidence.

Future evidence recovery should be a dated amendment preserving the limitations of
this publication. EREQ remains Candidate, AGBench Pilot v0.1 remains paused, and no
benchmark release, governance promotion or new experiment is implied by this note.
