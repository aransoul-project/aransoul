## Translation rule

Public documentation should use two layers where helpful:

1. **Native term** — preserves AranSoul's historical and poetic identity.
2. **Functional description** — states what the concept is intended to do in language that can be compared with other systems.

Neither layer should be used to imply empirical validation that has not occurred.
